Our Mission

We believe three revolutions are shaping the future — and we are building at their intersection.

Three revolutions

1

Open Source

We believe the code should be free and open. IT industry experience over the last decades shows that open source is one of the main drivers of progress. This revolution already has its shape — and we are committed to it fully.

2

Decentralized Blockchains

The revolution is happening and we are part of it. While many proofs for decentralised finance are in place, the final architecture is still unclear — and this is a great opportunity to take part in the DeFi landscape formation.

3

LLMs are sweeping pieces off the board

AI will allow people to truly become creators. We use AI to assist our work; we build AI to assist our users in becoming creators.
