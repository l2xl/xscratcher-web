The Scratcher Project is an open-source initiative building the infrastructure for innovative, creative and free IT tools — the kind that is missing today and that, we believe, many people are waiting for. The first of them: a trading terminal with a real trading H.U.D. and the AI assistant AI-Asassin.

Why we are building this

Exchange UIs are not built to make you win. Professional platforms want to be paid first and take months to learn. AI is changing everything — and today’s tools are not keeping up.

01

Trading is marginalized in the IT community

Millions of engineers could trade if the tools respected their skillset. Exchange UIs serve the exchange, not the trader; professional platforms charge before they teach.

02

The AI revolution is here

Everything that can be done on a computer will soon be done through AI. Current trading tools offer none of this — yet.

03

You sleep — the market does not

Crypto runs 24/7. A missed signal at night is a missed trade or an unprotected position. Manual monitoring is physically impossible.

04

Pay before you know if it works

Most solutions charge $15–100/month before delivering results. That barrier cuts off people who could become loyal users.

The roadmap

Three steps. Each one is a foundation for the next.

  1. 01 Done

    Foundation

    Already built and running: the data layer, the terminal’s trading H.U.D., and the AI assistant that now drives the project’s own development.

    • 📚
      DataHub library Unified data layer for market data aggregation, normalisation, and distribution across exchange connections.
    • ✈
      Trading HUD UI prototype A trading H.U.D.: chart, order book, positions, and orders on one screen. TA markers calculated automatically.
    • 🤖
      AI-Asassin prototype The Synergy Context Gate: the dialog condensed into a graph of statements that constructs the context for every reply, with a review and test gate. Open Trader is built with it.
    DataHub → Open Trader → AI-Asassin →
  2. 02 In development

    Automated trading

    Turning the prototype into a product that trades: orders that follow the market, strategies that run end to end, and a safe place to prove them first.

    • ⚡
      Dynamic orders Orders linked to indicators and updated in real time as the market moves.
    • ⚙
      Full stack automated trading Strategies executed end to end on your machine — from market data to orders on the exchange — with AI-Asassin building them and supervising their execution.
    • 🧪
      Trading strategy testbox Strategies proven on historical data and in a sandbox before they trade; parameter optimisation included.
    Full product details →
  3. 03 On the horizon

    Decentralised stack

    The logical conclusion of the stack: self-custody and decentralised settlement with the same Zero Trust principles — keys local, transactions transparent, code open.

    • 👛
      The Scratcher Web3 Wallet A Web3 self-custody wallet integrated with Open Trader and decentralised exchanges.
    • ⛓
      The Scratcher BlockChain Own blockchain for peer-to-peer settlement — no custodian, open source and independently auditable.
    • 🪙
      StableCoin The stack’s own stablecoin for settlement on the chain.
    • ⇄
      DEX A fully decentralised exchange with an on-chain order book.
