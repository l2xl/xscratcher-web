Problem & Solution

Exchange UIs are too simple; professional platforms too complex. Crypto runs 24/7. Most tools charge $15–100/month before delivering results — cutting off engineers who would become power users.

X·Cockpit is a free, open-source Trader Cockpit with an AI assistant that writes, tests, and runs strategies 24/7. No subscription barrier — we monetise through exchange commissions.

Business Model
Phase 1 From M10, revenue from trade #1 via the exchange API Broker ID — entry-tier fee share (~20–30% of fees), no minimum volume, on the user's own keys. Covers new and existing exchange users.
Phase 2 Tier rebate rises to up to 45–50% as volume grows; Broker Master (~mo. 22–24) unlocks a broker sub-account for 24/7 cloud execution. AI assistant $50/mo + $5/mo per extra exchange.
