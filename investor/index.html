Market Opportunity

Global crypto trading fee TAM: $1.63B, growing at 16% CAGR. Low regulation, high volatility, and low trade-volume competition create an ideal entry window.

SAM: 1–2M algorithmic traders — engineers who already know crypto, discover tools on GitHub, and trust open-source. SAM potential at 5%: 100 000+ traders · ARR $480M+.

Problem & Solution

Exchange UIs are too simple; professional platforms too complex. Crypto runs 24/7. Most tools charge $15–100/month before delivering results — cutting off engineers who would become power users.

X·Cockpit is a free, open-source Trader Cockpit with an AI assistant that writes, tests, and runs strategies 24/7. No subscription barrier — we monetise through exchange commissions.

Business Model
Phase 1 Exchange pays 20–30% of commission via Broker ID. Revenue from trade #1 — no trader subscription, no minimum threshold.
Phase 2 Fee share grows to 40–50% at $10M+/mo volume. Cloud Starter $12/mo (24/7 AI execution). Marketplace 10–20% cut on strategy sales.
