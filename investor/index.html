Problem

Retail traders have no single trading tool that is convenient, free and secure

Only ~8% of crypto holders trade actively.1 Among the rest are many technically skilled users who need only one thing to start: a convenient tool.

Exchange notation from the pre-computer era
The current UX is clumsy to use; professional platforms are overloaded and demand a steep learning curve.
AI tools haven't reached maturity yet
The industry is still working out what AI assistants will be — this niche is open for us.
A double barrier: cost & trust
Pay blindly before any result — and hand your API keys to a third-party service.
