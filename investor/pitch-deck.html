X·Cockpit Open-source AI platform for crypto trading
◆ Pre-Seed · Confidential
Problem

No open-source crypto-trading app uses the full power of the modern computer and AI

01
No comfortable middle ground
Exchange UIs lack functionality; professional platforms offer depth for specialists.
02
AI answers — nothing orchestrates the work
Claude answers beside the chart, but nothing orchestrates the work around it — it all stays on the trader.
03
The trader sleeps — the market doesn't
A signal missed overnight = a trade lost or a position left unguarded. Crypto runs 24/7.
04
Speed decides
While the trader hand-calculates SL/TP, the setup has already moved. Every manual click is a built-in loss.
05
A double barrier: cost & trust
$15–100/mo subscriptions before any result — plus handing API keys to a third-party service.
Solution

X·Cockpit — the cockpit + an AI assistant

A free, open-source trader cockpit with an AI assistant that writes, tests and runs strategies 24/7. No subscription barrier — we monetise through exchange commissions.

1 · X·Cockpit UI — one interface to every CEX
A reimagined trader's interface — as powerful as a pro tool yet intuitive, with automation built into the UI itself.
2 · AI Assistant — the trader's co-pilot
Wired into the platform — it sees positions and supervises execution. It orchestrates the work around the trader. Tasks and decisions stay with the trader; the assistant runs the rest.
3 · Server-side automation that never sleeps
Strategies and dynamic orders run 24/7 on our backend.
4 · Advanced orders out of the box
Maximum automation means no opportunity lost to the time otherwise spent setting orders up by hand.
5 · Open Source + keys never with us
Transparent code, zero entry barrier — users see what works before they pay. API keys stay with the user or the exchange, never with us.
Product

The trader's interface, reimagined

X·Cockpit rethinks the trader's interface end to end — from how the chart is visualised to how orders are placed — to take the maximum from the computer: the most automation possible, built into the interface itself. The four below are early examples. MVP in active development.

Auto-TA markers
Support, resistance and trends drawn the moment price moves.
Dynamic orders
SL/TP lock onto the TA markers and recompute themselves as the market moves.
New order types
Dynamic limit, Catch the Tail and orders unavailable on the exchange itself.
Order book synced to the chart
Market depth on the price axis — order flow at a glance.
AI assistant
Helps write and optimise strategies and orchestrates the work around the trader. Semi-automatic mode: the trader decides, the AI builds the strategy, supervises execution and picks the entry moment.

Insight

The limit is attention, not capital

Traders rarely split large deposits — running many positions at once is operationally hard. X·Cockpit makes it practical.

Same deposit, more turnover — tooling fragments & grows volume
X·Cockpit lets a trader run dozens of positions 24/7 → higher turnover on the same deposit → + fee share.
