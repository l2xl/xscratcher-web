Problem

Manual trading still runs on pre-computer rules

01
Exchange UIs are stuck in the past
Too basic to trade in; pro platforms (Hummingbot, HaasOnline) are overloaded. No middle ground for a serious retail trader.
02
AI in trading is a bolt-on, not a tool
Today's solutions put an AI chat next to the chart. Nobody ships an AI that sees positions, writes strategies and supervises execution.
03
The trader sleeps — the market doesn't
A signal missed overnight = a trade lost or a position left unguarded. Crypto runs 24/7.
04
Speed decides — clicks cost money
While the trader hand-calculates SL/TP, the setup has already moved. Every manual click is a built-in loss.
05
A double barrier: cost & trust
$15–100/mo subscriptions before any result — plus handing API keys to a third-party service. 3Commas, 2022 — $22M of customer losses.
Solution

X·Cockpit — the cockpit + an AI assistant

A free, open-source trader cockpit with an AI assistant that writes, tests and runs strategies 24/7. No subscription barrier — we monetise through exchange commissions.

1 · X·Cockpit UI — one interface to every CEX
A reimagined trader's interface, with maximum automation built into the UI itself.
2 · AI Assistant — a staff-grade assistant
Helps write and optimise strategies. Semi-automatic mode: the trader decides, the AI builds the strategy, supervises execution and picks the entry moment.
3 · Server-side automation that never sleeps
Strategies and dynamic orders run 24/7 on our backend. “AI works at night, the human reviews in the morning” — a bridge between manual and blind automation.
4 · Advanced orders out of the box
Dynamic orders recompute automatically. The position is protected against loss the moment it opens — no chasing SL/TP by hand.
5 · Open Source + keys never with us
Transparent code, zero entry barrier — users see what works before they pay. API keys stay with the user or the exchange, never with us — local when self-hosted, on a broker sub-account in the cloud.
Product

The trader's interface, reimagined

X·Cockpit rethinks the trader's interface end to end — from how the chart is visualised to how orders are placed — to take the maximum from the computer: the most automation possible, built into the interface itself. The four below are early examples. MVP in active development.

Auto-TA markers
Support, resistance and trends drawn the moment price moves — no manual redraw.
Dynamic orders
SL/TP lock onto your TA markers and recompute themselves as the market moves.
New order types
Dynamic limit, Catch the Tail and orders you can’t place on the exchange itself.
Order book on the price axis
Market depth aligned to the chart, so you read order flow at a glance.

Business Model

The user pays nothing — the exchange pays us

From the trader's first trade (M10), X·Cockpit earns a share of the exchange trading fee via the API Broker ID — entry tier ~20–30%, rising to up to 45–50% as volume grows. No subscription, no minimum volume; works on the user's own keys, covering new and existing users.
