Problem

Manual trading still runs on pre-computer rules

01
Exchange UIs are stuck in the past
Too basic to trade in; pro platforms (Hummingbot, HaasOnline) are overloaded. No middle ground for a serious retail trader.
02
AI in trading is a bolt-on, not a tool
Today's AI sits in a window next to the chart — it isn't integrated into the trading tool itself.
03
The trader sleeps — the market doesn't
A signal missed overnight = a trade lost or a position left unguarded. Crypto runs 24/7.
04
Speed decides
While the trader hand-calculates SL/TP, the setup has already moved. Every manual click is a built-in loss.
05
A double barrier: cost & trust
$15–100/mo subscriptions before any result — plus handing API keys to a third-party service.
Solution

X·Cockpit — the cockpit + an AI assistant

A free, open-source trader cockpit with an AI assistant that writes, tests and runs strategies 24/7. No subscription barrier — we monetise through exchange commissions.

1 · X·Cockpit UI — one interface to every CEX
A reimagined trader's interface — as powerful as a pro tool yet intuitive, with automation built into the UI itself.
2 · AI Assistant
An AI wired into the platform: it sees your positions and the order book, and supervises execution.
3 · Server-side automation that never sleeps
Strategies and dynamic orders run 24/7 on our backend.
4 · Advanced orders out of the box
Maximum automation means no opportunity lost to the time you'd otherwise spend setting orders up by hand.
5 · Open Source + keys never with us
Transparent code, zero entry barrier — users see what works before they pay. API keys stay with the user or the exchange, never with us.
Product

The trader's interface, reimagined

X·Cockpit rethinks the trader's interface end to end — from how the chart is visualised to how orders are placed — to take the maximum from the computer: the most automation possible, built into the interface itself. The four below are early examples. MVP in active development.

Auto-TA markers
Support, resistance and trends drawn the moment price moves — no manual redraw.
Dynamic orders
SL/TP lock onto your TA markers and recompute themselves as the market moves.
New order types

Order book on the price axis
Market depth aligned to the chart, so you read order flow at a glance.
AI assistant

Unit Economics

The unit is one active trader — and it compounds

Our unit of economics is a single active user: a trader who has connected exchange keys and trades through X·Cockpit. We earn a share of their exchange fee, so the metric that drives everything is ARPAU — average monthly revenue per active user. Every trader sits in one of four growth cohorts, defined by deposit size and trading maturity.

Four cohorts — a trader's growth ladder
A · Beginning
$2–5K deposit. First steps — small size, occasional trades, learning the cockpit and the market.
B · Progressing
$5–15K deposit. Gaining confidence — trades regularly, leans on alerts and dynamic orders.
C · Advancing
$15–25K deposit. Semi-pro — runs multi-leg setups and lets the assistant test & automate strategies.
D · Mastering
$30K+ deposit. Power trader — high volume, full 24/7 automation across exchanges.
The mix shifts upward — the product pulls traders up the ladder
User mix at launch · M0–6
User mix at maturity · M36

As the assistant tests and runs strategies 24/7, automation lifts trading volume and visual control cuts mistakes, traders trade more and grow their deposits — so the heavy Beginning base (55%→38%) steadily migrates into Progressing, Advancing and Mastering. This upward drift is a direct result of the product's advantages, not paid acquisition — and it is what compounds ARPAU over time.
