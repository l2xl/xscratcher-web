Open Trader Open-source AI platform for crypto trading
Problem

Retail traders have no single crypto-trading tool that's convenient, free to start, secure, and comes with an AI assistant

Only ~8% of crypto holders trade actively — 92% of exchange users don't.1 Among them are technically skilled, analytically minded users who need only one thing to start: a convenient, universal tool.

Exchange notation from the pre-computer era
Exchange notation doesn't tap the power of the modern computer and is clumsy to use; professional platforms are overloaded and demand long learning and a developer's skillset.
AI trading tools haven't reached maturity
AI in crypto trading is still a young, emerging category — the niche is open for us to claim our place.
A double barrier: cost & trust
Pay blindly before any result — and hand your API keys to a third-party service.
1 Coinbase: 115M users, ~9.2M transacting monthly (~8%). Business of Apps; Backlinko.
Solution

Open Trader — the trader terminal + an AI assistant

A free, open-source trading terminal with an AI assistant. No paywall to start — we monetise through exchange commissions; the 24/7 AI-assistant cloud tier is an optional subscription.

Trader HUD — one interface to every centralized exchange
A reimagined trader's interface — as powerful as a pro tool yet intuitive, with automation built into the UI itself.
AI Assistant — the trader's co-pilot
Wired into the platform — it sees positions and supervises execution. It orchestrates the work around the trader. Tasks and decisions stay with the trader; the assistant runs the rest.
Advanced orders out of the box
A position is protected against loss the moment it opens — no chasing price with manual SL/TP.
Server-side automation that never sleeps
Strategies and dynamic orders run 24/7 on our backend.
Open Source + keys never with us
Transparent code, zero entry barrier — users see what works before they pay. API keys stay with the user or the exchange, never with us.
