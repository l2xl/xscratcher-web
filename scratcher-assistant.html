02 — Planning

The Scratcher Assistant

An AI assistant orchestrating you trades Writes, debugs, and optimises your strategies — and it never sleeps.

The AI revolution is starting — right now

In the short term, everything that can be done on a computer will be done through AI or with AI assistance. The gap between what AI can already do and what trading tools provide is widening every month.

LLMs are sweeping pieces off the board. AI will allow people to truly become creators. We use AI to assist our work; we build AI to assist our users in becoming creators.

What The Scratcher Assistant does

The Scratcher Assistant helps you write and optimise automated trading strategies, gives advice, simplifies automation, and — unlike you — does not sleep.

💻
Strategy development

Write strategy code from a natural-language description. The assistant generates, explains, and iterates on the implementation until it matches your intent.

🔧
Parameter optimisation

Tune strategy parameters against historical data. The assistant interprets backtest results and suggests adjustments with clear reasoning.

🔍
Result analysis

Every recommendation is logged and explainable. No opaque decisions, no black-box outputs — you always understand why.

🌙
Always on

The market runs 24/7 and so does The Scratcher Assistant. It monitors, advises, and alerts while you are away.

Integration with The Exchange Scratcher

The Scratcher Assistant integrates directly with The Exchange Scratcher through an open MCP (Model Context Protocol) interface, giving it access to live market data, backtest results, and your strategy configuration. The assistant sees what you see — and helps you act on it.
